\section{Mathematical Optimization}


\subsection{Exact Methods}

    Including available solving tools, such as CPLEX and Gurobi. These methods guarantee optimality but may require significant computational resources for large instances.

\subsection{Heuristic Methods}

    Discussing heuristic methods, which provide good solutions in reasonable time frames but do not guarantee optimality. Examples include genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, and tabu search.

\subsection{Relaxation Techniques}

    Discussing relaxation techniques, such as linear programming relaxation, which can provide bounds and insights into the problem structure.
